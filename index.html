About Akanksha Cosmetics

Akanksha Cosmetics started as a college startup concept — an attempt to take a handful of ingredients long used in home hair-care routines and shape them into one simple, everyday spray.

There's no laboratory and no long history behind the name yet. What we have is a small team, a botanical-inspired formulation, and an interest in keeping the product honest about what it is: a prototype, built to be questioned and improved.

  • Student innovation. Built as a college project, from ingredient sourcing to packaging concept.
  • Nature-inspired formulation. Centred on rosemary, clove and methi.
  • Simplicity. One product, one clear use, no unnecessary steps.
  • Transparency. We say what's confirmed and label what isn't, yet.

Our Story

A Simple Idea. A Natural Beginning.

It started with a simple thought — hair care shouldn't feel complicated.

With so many hair-care products and endless promises around them, we wanted to explore a simpler approach: a convenient product inspired by familiar botanical ingredients and traditional hair-care routines.

That thought became Akanksha Cosmetics — a student startup concept built around rosemary, clove, and methi, brought together in an easy-to-use spray format.

We wanted Akanksha Cosmetics to feel simple, approachable, and honest — a product idea that connects nature-inspired care with everyday routines.

For us, Akanksha Cosmetics isn't just about creating a product. It's about taking a small idea and giving it the chance to bloom.
